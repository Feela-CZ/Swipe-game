# Episode I — curated visual reference set 04: equipment

Internal art library only. Promote individual clean exports later; never crop an entire reference sheet into the game.

## Approved equipment directions

- **Shields:** clear round, kite, tower and ceremonial silhouettes; wood-and-iron for common, heraldic color for uncommon, then carefully escalating magic for high rarities.
- **Rings:** strong small-item language for luck, warding, elemental, celestial, nature and cursed effects. Keep the setting legible at icon scale.
- **Amulets:** excellent material for named effects and factional rewards — sun, moon, tree, wolf, feather, hourglass, eye, rune and saint motifs.
- **Relics:** reserve the most distinctive objects for story rewards, bosses and meaningful discoveries. Examples include clockwork relics, maps, sealed books, saintly icons, rune stones and bottles.

## Blunt weapon warning

The mace, hammer and flail sheet is **not approved for direct use**. Several weapons have malformed chain connections, impossible striking heads, inconsistent handles or decorative parts that make no mechanical sense. It may inform the broad material palette only.

When creating a blunt weapon later, validate: one readable striking head, one continuous haft or handle, a plausible grip, and for a flail exactly one visibly attached chain from handle to head. Avoid random spikes, duplicate heads and ornamental clutter.

## Rarity discipline

Bright aura, animated crystal, crown motifs and extreme ornament belong to Epic/Legendary or a named quest item. Common and Rare equipment must remain materially grounded, or item rarity stops being visually meaningful.
