# Episode I — curated visual reference set 01

These source images are kept as an internal art library, not shipped in the web build. They establish the target look: warm, storybook fantasy rendered with tactile metal, worn wood, blue arcane light, and selective crimson accents.

## Approved anchors

- **King Overtime / Král Přesčas** — the red-and-gold, clock-bearing ruler is the visual anchor for Episode I. Reuse the seated paperwork pose for story interludes, the pointing pose for threats, and the staff pose for the boss encounter.
- **Creatures** — slimes, the basilisk-like lizard, gargoyle, lion-serpent, burrowing worm, frog and armored spider are useful bestiary directions. They must enter only where their habitat and story beat make sense.
- **Encounter props** — wells, shrines, bridges, ruined camps, sealed rune doors, portals, a merchant cart, marked maps, nests, and ritual sites make strong readable encounter landmarks.

## Deliberate exclusions

- Do not use dragons or other late-game-scale creatures in Episode I merely because an image is available.
- Avoid the inconsistent creature variants and any art with unclear silhouettes.
- Keep graphic gore out of routine encounters. The bloodier camps, corpses, stakes, and execution imagery are references only until a specific serious story moment earns them.
- The obvious bear trap, noose, and other punishment imagery should never be used as decorative filler; they need a coherent event and a player choice.

## Integration rule

When an asset is promoted into the game, export or generate a clean single-subject scene asset with the same lighting and finish. Never crop a whole concept sheet directly into the player experience.
