# Episode I — curated visual reference set 02

Internal reference library only. Keep the same rendered storybook-fantasy finish as set 01: strong silhouettes, tactile material detail, warm highlights, restrained crimson, and clear readable poses.

## Approved directions

- **Wild threats:** rat, bat, spider, scorpion, serpent, raven, ferret, boar, wolf, lizard, beetle, toad and centipede. Use them only in believable habitats and scale their menace to the route.
- **Undead:** basic skeleton guards, armored skeletons, spectral mourners, wraiths and one necromancer-type encounter are good directions for tower or crypt content.
- **Raiders:** melee goblins, shield bearers, scouts, rogue-like human adversaries and heavy brutes are usable when their faction and equipment match the episode story.
- **Friendly encounters:** bard, halfling traveller, gnome tinkerer, dwarf, priestess, caravan traveller, monk, water spirit, tree guardian and pack dog are promising character seeds.

## Excluded from use

- **Every archer reference in this sheet is rejected.** The bows, strings, grip, arrow placement, and/or pose are not credible enough for the finished game. Do not crop, reuse or derive archery art from them.
- The most graphic undead variants are not default enemies; reserve horror imagery for a deliberate later location.
- Do not use the fairy, elemental or high-fantasy figures as arbitrary decoration. They need an actual story role.

## Story fit notes

The raven can become a recurring watcher of the king's bureaucracy. The gnome and dwarf fit repairs, mining or clockwork problems. The bard and traveller suit optional social decisions. The tree guardian and water spirit belong in later forest or river branches, not in the tower by default.
