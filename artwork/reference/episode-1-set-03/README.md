# Episode I — curated visual reference set 03: chests

Internal art library only. These are reference sheets, never direct in-game crops.

## Chest hierarchy to preserve

1. **Common** — worn oak, iron bands, simple lock. The normal chest; no fireworks.
2. **Guarded / uncommon** — heavy iron, stone, moss or a small rune lock. Use for a place-specific reward or an optional risk.
3. **Rare** — blue rune glow, a travel pack, carefully made gold trim or crystals. Clear but restrained promise of a better reward.
4. **Epic** — ornate sun, royal red-gold, dragon, or strongly magical construction. Rare enough that its presentation can breathe.
5. **Legendary / story** — a chest visually tied to the king, an episode boss or a one-off relic. Never a random palette swap.

## Mimic rule

The toothy mimic is a deliberately readable enemy, not a cheap indistinguishable trap. It will later appear as a rare chest encounter with an early visual tell, a choice to inspect/open/leave, and a meaningful reward if defeated. Do not make ordinary chests secretly mimic-like by default.

## Exclusions

- Avoid overly busy effects that conceal the chest silhouette.
- The heart, sea, ice and dragon variants need a future biome or story context; they are not generic Episode I drops.
