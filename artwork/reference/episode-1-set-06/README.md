# Episode I — curated visual reference set 06: physical melee equipment

Internal art library only. This set establishes the initial equipment scope.

## Current release constraint

Until the combat and item systems explicitly expand, equipment and enemies use **physical melee only**:

- allowed: swords, daggers, axes, shields, armor, gloves, boots, and grounded blunt weapons rebuilt from validated shapes;
- deferred: staves, magical foci, elemental weapons, rune-glowing blades, bows, crossbows and all other ranged weapons.

Deferred does not mean discarded. It means the assets stay archived until the game has mechanics and story support for them.

## Approved visual directions

- **Armor:** leather, padded cloth, mail, brigandine and practical plate make a clear progression from Common to Rare. Heraldic and elaborate sets belong to Epic/Legendary or faction rewards.
- **Gloves and boots:** the practical leather and mail sets are ideal early gear. Claws, extreme spikes, frost, flame and angelic trim need specific traits or late rarity.
- **Daggers:** use clean, readable single-bladed forms for normal loot. Curved or decorated blades are acceptable only when their silhouette remains physically plausible.

## Quality filters

- Do not promote any weapon that looks magical solely because the sheet contains it.
- Validate every sword, axe and dagger as a usable object: one coherent blade, handle and guard; no floating parts or accidental duplicate edges.
- Preserve visual rarity discipline. Material quality and small heraldic details should precede glowing effects.
